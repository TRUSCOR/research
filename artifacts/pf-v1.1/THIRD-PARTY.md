# Third-party data and terms

No third-party raw data is redistributed in this artifact, with two small
exceptions, both under AgentDojo's MIT licence (notice below): the AgentDojo
user-task texts that differ between published logs (18 tasks), quoted in
`pf-agentdojo-supplementary-2026-11-12.json`, and one published run log
(`runs/gpt-4o-2024-05-13/banking/user_task_1/important_instructions/injection_task_0.json`),
converted into the trace `01-gpt-4o-2024-published.sova-trace` in `findings/`. Otherwise the studies read the sources
below at pinned versions, and the result files contain only counts, digests,
identifiers, categorical labels, and derived metrics.
